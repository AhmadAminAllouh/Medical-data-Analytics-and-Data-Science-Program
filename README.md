# Medical-data-Analytics-and-Data-Science-Program
In this project, I utilized medical data from individuals to study the factors influencing whether or not they attend their appointments, and subsequently built a model to predict the outcomes.
With this dataset, I encountered a genuine challenge common in classification tasks: the influence of external factors not captured in the data—such as traffic congestion or delays due to family circumstances—on the outcome. Since these factors are not present in the dataset, the model cannot account for them. To address this, I employed the best methods and most suitable models, as demonstrated in the code.

Data source: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/joniarroba/noshowappointments/code
